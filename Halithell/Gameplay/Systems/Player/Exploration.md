
<h1><center>Exploration</center></h1>

***

This document contains all exploration-specific systems available to the player. It does *not* take into account progression order, and instead details the *total* amount of exploration systems that will be available throughout the game.

### Black Dahlia
The **Black Dahlia** is a special kind of [[Nahline]] flower that will react strongly to **Umbra**, rapidly growing large when she interacts with it, and serving as a bed where she can replenish her stats, and reform on death.

Activating a **Dahlia** for the first time gives **Umbra** a small boost to her [Health](Combat.md#Attributes) and [Power](Combat.md#Attributes).

>[!info]
>The [[Dahliantis]] is a special type of praying mantis that assumes the form of a **Black Dahlia**. They only exist within [Eiare](Eiare.md) due to the influence of [[Orthurys]].


### Qualia Droplets
**Qualia Droplets** are condensed units of **Physical Qualia** which may be collected by **Umbra**. They can be found in the world, obtained by defeating enemies, or from alternative sources. **Umbra** has individual **Droplet Containers** for each type of **Qualia**, except **Nihilium**, which she is *not* compatible with.

They are used as an ingredient in the creation and/or activation of [[Injectors]], as well as relevant portions of quests.

### Alliciency
[Umbra](Umbra.md) automatically absorbs [Qualia](Qualia.md) from the environment, as well as from naturally interacting with enemies and allies alike. Her absorption does *not* affect the energy levels of objects or entities in any noticeable way.

> [!note]
After activating sufficient **Dahlias**, **Umbra's** [Energy](Combat.md#Attributes) gauge starts slowly rising automatically.
