Eiare used to be a lush, sprawling world full of biological and philosophical diversity. There were many conscious humanoid clans who formed settlements, and some of these clans prospered exponentially. In the Graeme region, there were some smaller clans up in the Malicis mountains (the Niandes clan in Mt. Edivius, the Maylith clan in the depths of the Exulas Highlands, and the newly formed Saeris clan living in the Veralis region on the surface of Exulas), but down below was the massive metropolis of Elphira, nestled within the Malicis Gorges, inhabited and built by the highly advanced Esteria clan. This was one of their many advanced settlements.

Further away, in the Calmerra region, lived the Avalians. The Avalia and Esteria clans had always been fundamentally opposed in their philosophies, but they lived at peace with one another. Esterians believed in their craft of "Artifiction;" they wished to understand the world mechanically, through observation and experimentation, and used this capability to create wonderful inventions, leading to technological innovations such as the Clockwork Revolution.

Avalians, on the other hand, believed in the Spirit of Nature. They believed Nature was divine in its essence, and ought to be left to its usual wiles, unperturbed. They saw Artifiction as dangerous, and sought to uphold Nature and their Natural Order.

They deified this Spirit of Nature by calling her Auris, and, one day, a nascent babe with golden locks was left at the doorstep to one of the Avalian cathedrals.

This child boasted extraordinary abilities and a magnanimous character, and was believed by many to be the physical incarnation of Auris. As such, that became her name.

Life on Eiare was relatively peaceful, until the start of the Age of Calamities. Natural disasters struck one after the other. Terrible storms, earthquakes, pests, plagues. The Esterians were baffled, as none of their machinery predicted these sudden extreme shifts in weather. Esterian Astronomy was still in its infancy, and looking up at the night sky revealed little difference from before. Similarly they could find no evidence on Eiare for the cause, other than that the levels of "Anima" (what they call Qualia) in the environment appear to be perturbed from their usual readings.

These calamities claimed many lives, and Avalia became increasingly convinced that it was the Esterians who were angering Auris. Auris, the person, was alive at this time, and had absolutely no issue with the Esterians. However, Avalians believed this was a result of her good nature as a human, and that the rest of her spirit was suffering from Esterian Artifiction.

Thus a war ensued, and it was fierce. Where Esterians had advanced capabilities, Avalians had sheer numbers, and fierce fighting power still. In their raw devotion to Auris, some Avalians had still managed to develop Qualia-based abilities, which they called Benedictions.

This combination made them formidable enemies on the battlefield. But Esterians managed to hold their own. Casualties piled up, and many places started becoming uninhabitable. Floods wracked the lowlands of Calmerra, and Avalians started moving to higher ground, as small victories gave them control over bits of Graemen land.

The worse the floods got, the more the population dwindled. The first to go on both sides were the men, as they were fighting the wars and protecting the women and children. Many brilliant Esterian Artificers and Researchers remained, and they formulated a plan to at least counteract the severe loss of population: Hybrids.

Esterian genetic technology was quite advanced, and, after much work, they were successfully able to create a human using only DNA from a single gender; but it was not a clone. Instead, the supplanting DNA was provided from other animals, creating a human-animal hybrid. The first successful one was a mix between a bat, a badger, and a human.

She was named Mellivora Vansonia, and spent most of her early life in a lab. Her appearance was that of a beastly human, and many Esterians found her fearsome to behold. As a result, Mellivora grew disdainful and cynical.

Mellivora had bat wings, dark and leathery bat-like skin, a bushy badger tail, and fierce, red, oval irises. While Esterians were fairly "scientific" thanks to their mindset around Artifiction, they still had a very hard time accepting Mellivora. She did not look natural, and was additionally absurdly strong.

Hybrids were engineered to be resilient, and the fact they were created with Anima (Qualia) meant they had an incredible aptitude for it. Their other unique trait is that they are able to procreate without the need for a male mate; rather, they can procreate at will, as long as they ingest DNA of a mate in some form. This was done as a deliberate choice, to ensure the continued survival of the species even if all the men were to die out.

Once the Avalians caught wind of Mellivora, they came to believe she was the manifestation of evil. They believed Esterians wished to do away with the Nature of Auris, and instead replace it with Artifiction, and that Mellivora was created as a foil to the physical manifestation of Auris.

This amplified the war, at the same time as the natural disasters intensified. It all came to a head when Auris was dispatched to deal with Mellivora herself, blaming the intensity of the disasters on her.

Esterians had been reluctant to utilise Mellivora in the war, as she wasn't particularly loyal to them, but stayed with them mostly out of an absence of anywhere else to go. Mellivora, however, wasn't too keen on having her only safe environment rattled with, so she defended of her own volition; and fiercely so.

Auris, meanwhile, was fairly confused in all this. She did not believe the Esterians were causing the natural disasters, but the Avalian Retinue did not listen to her, claiming her to be poisoned by her mortal mind. She allowed herself to be dispatched in an attempt to converse with Mellivora to get to the bottom of this.

From an outside perspective, they waged a battle of epic proportions. Eventually, both of them distanced themselves from the battlefield, and vanished. Less than an hour after that, there was a massive explosion in the distance, and the calamities receded soon after. Auris and Mellivora were never seen or heard from again.

Alace was found in a basket many, many years later, after the water levels had kept rising to the point of turning the Exulas Highlands into no more than a set of islands. Avalia and Esteria built new settlements on what land remained, and the war waged on. Esterians were now primarily Hybrids, as they had perfected the technology that had created Mellivora.

Alace was homed by the Calverrs; two humble adoptive parents who received her anonymously after hearing a knock and checking, but finding no one around but the young girl. She was placed in a small basket (with a card attached with her name on it, as well as a small set of instructions) wearing a pendant that seemed a family heirloom. 

They were of meagre means, but they cared for her as their own.

By now, the war had died down, and few battles still took place. The Esterian Aeltor family, renowned for their technological marvels, had invented a line of military automatons (the AMOS units) which were able to hold off any attempted Avalian invasions; and without Auris or big numbers, Avalians couldn't afford to keep sending soldiers over nothing.

Alace grew up in this tense, pseudo-war environment, in the slums of the Divine City annexing Veralis and Exulas. She had extraordinary strength; at only six years old, her strength exceeded that of veteran soldiers. She was also scrappy and rambunctious, which got her in trouble with law enforcement more than once; until one day, the High Commander of the Avalian Royal Guard took notice. At only ten years old, she was offered the opportunity to become an Avalian Knight, if she accepted the years of training that preceded it. With the encouragement of her parents (particularly Lace Calverr, her adoptive mother), she accepted.

She was taken to the Pristine Castle, where she was trained in the art of combat. While she received training in a variety of weapons, she was abysmal at most of them (particularly archery) and excelled with a sword.

She quickly developed a reputation for being a tough child, and as she grew up, her tenacity in a battle led to her gaining the title "The Unwavering." She was rarely known to so much as budge, and never known to lose.

As she grew up, the calamities once again returned, and Avalians blamed Esterians anew. They blamed the existence of Hybrids, and so the war was reignited. Once Alace joined the fray, she managed to make quick work of the Esterian automatons, and Avalians earned a decisive victory.

For playing such a pivotal role in the victory, Alace was granted the title of Divine General, and took up permanent residence in the Pristine Castle, as well as earning the Calverr family a spot in the Residential District.

It wasn't until Umbra eventually managed to knock Alace out (by smashing her through the floor of the Pristine Castle) that it was revealed (not only to Umbra and Alace, but the entire Holy Retinue, who happened to reside in those chambers) that Alace is, in fact, a Hybrid.

Getting blown out of her armour, Alace's pendant had scattered, and it was clear to everyone that she had massive bat ears, and a badger's tail. The revered Divine General was a Hybrid. 

The Retinue was disgusted with her, and claimed her to be an Esterian spy. They almost immediately offered the Divine General role to Umbra, who was a little unsure what to do in this situation. Upon awakening, even Alace was surprised that she was a Hybrid; but she was even more shocked by the sudden disdain and disregard she was receiving from the Retinue.

Their vitriol and disregard for all her efforts and contributions to the Avalian people quickly escalated, and sent Alace into a fit of rage, whereupon she ripped the entire Pristine Castle out of the ground, dragged it into the sky, and shattered it into pieces.

As it turns out, the Avalians were not correct about Auris, the cause of the calamities, or Mellivora.

Initially, upon seeing Auris on the battlefield, Mellivora immediately attacked her -- believing she was the biggest threat. However, Auris played pure defense and evasion, asking questions until Mellivora caved and responded.

Upon learning the truth; that Mellivora was created as a means to combat extinction, and that all the Esterians found was unusual Anima readings, Auris herself took Mellivora with her, in the hopes of finding the cause and settling this, once and for all.

Mellivora had already done a lot of her own investigation, because the calamities, according to Esterian data, all seemed to originate from the ocean.

Upon further investigation, they ended up discovering Neketh in the midst of one of his rampages. Neketh, the Kethra of Eiare, had been severely disrupted by the Annexing of Orthurys and Sallerys.

Mellivora and Auris both engaged the giant, 7-eyed sea serpent together, and a valiant battle ensued, which culminated in the beast being subdued, but Mellivora ended up having her wings ripped and back broken. Having become immobile due to her severe injuries, she told Auris to leave her there. But Auris chose to stay and care for Mellivora instead.

Auris and Mellivora grew increasingly fond of each other over the next several months, and eventually had a child -- something made possible by the unique genetics of Hybrids -- and they initially planned to raise this child together.

However, when little Alace was close to two years old, Neketh began rampaging again. Orthurys and Sallerys had finally grown their Verities to the point of full consciousness, and this increased the disturbance in their Annexes severely, causing the beast to go mad. 

Auris left to fight it on her own this time, and, whilst having put a valiant effort, found herself with her back against the wall against this eldritch beast. As a last resort, she put all of her energy into subduing the beast with her powerful blade; the Temporum Fetish known as Erathel.

In doing so, she sacrificed her own life, but left herself behind as a Remnant, to eternally subdue the beast.

Mellivora grieved the loss of her loved one, but was faced with another issue; she could not raise Alace on her own. She had recovered a lot from her injuries, but was incapable of protecting the child from danger, should the situation arise.

She resorted to return to what was left of the mainland, and contact a member of the Niandes tribe. Auris, in her lifetime, had made many friends; and one she spoke of often was Shendala, the Great Orator of Niandes. 

Mellivora came to Shendala and told her everything. Shendala sympathised with them, and mourned the loss of her friend as well. She understood the need for Alace to be cared for, but couldn't do so herself; as the Orator, she was required to frequently be near dangerous places, and she lived within Mt. Edivius itself, which had become largely inhospitable since Neketh's deterioration, triply so for a young toddler.

Instead, she fashioned a Memorium Fetish; a pendant which Alace could wear, which would mask her Hybrid features from outside observers. It drew its energy from her consciousness, so she had to be awake for the pendant to work; but it would allow her to be raised by one of the Avalians in the Divine City, which was currently the safest place Alace could be.

Mellivora entrusted Alace with Shendala, having faith in Auris herself saying she'd trust Shendala with her life. Shendala ended up finding a lovely couple; one she knew would have no issues with Alace's animal features, even if they were to find out. She left Alace at their doorstep, and the rest of the story unfolded.
