### Backstory
Hakti is the last remaining survivor of the Saeris Clan; a group of Amazons that embraced Shaman knowledge and formed a hybrid fighting style that focused on utilizing the elements in conjunction with melee combat.

Whilst they cared about combat greatly due to descending from Amazons, the primary purpose of their departure was because they sought to be more peaceful. They viewed combat as a last resort and, despite their extensive training, generally tried avoiding it outside of sparring matches.

The Saeris were a very friendly and tolerant people, and Hakti was a dedicated, albeit unremarkable clan member hailing from an unremarkable family. She worked hard and cared a lot about her peers, and was generally well-respected within her community. She was very social, but very clumsy; and while everyone treated her kindly, she struggled forming any real connections with anyone but her neighbor and best friend Luscerna - whom she referred to as "Lucy."

Unlike Hakti, Lucy seemed to make friends with everyone she met; she was witty, kind, and energetic; always able to adapt herself to whatever situation she found herself in, or squeeze her way out of tricky situations. Despite their differences, though, Lucy was extremely fond of Hakti, often saying she wished she had the ability to be herself like Hakti was, rather than pretending to be someone else just so others would like her.

While Saeris had a hierarchy, they always made it a point to treat everyone as equals, no matter their position within the Clan. They were also extremely hospitable to any outsiders, and would occasionally even welcome others into the Clan; however, being a part of the Clan was seen as a life-long commitment, and one would have to prove this dedication before being allowed official membership. Even Saeris descendants were not exempt from this.

Once inaugurated into the Clan, its symbol would be tattooed upon their forehead in a henna-like tint; with slight alterations depending on their specialty.

The Saeris Clan members were viewed as barbaric heretics by the Avalian Holy Retinue; for welcoming Hybrids into their clan, making use of Esterian technology, being descendants of Amazons, and making use of unholy Shamanic magic.

For this crime, their Clan was sentenced to exile, and their leaders were sentenced to death. However, due to Auris' Divine Edicts, they were forbidden from enacting punishments on juveniles; thus anyone aged 15 or under was imprisoned instead, and had their "unholy markings" removed by scorching them off.

Despite this Edict, a lot of Hakti's peers still wound up dying; either due to excessive physical abuse leading to "unintended" deaths (there is no Edict preventing "disciplining" juveniles), or due to starvation or medical malpractice. This was also paired with Saeris members that turned 16 and were often exiled to the Path of Love; which was functionally a death sentence.

Hakti was no exception to the abuse, and she watched a lot of her friends die, which deteriorated her mental health and caused her to start having delusions and hallucinations; seeing her dead friends and family appearing before her, either mangled or in pain, every single day, and regularly suffering nightmares that relived those experiences, or experiences of her being abused.

She had always been able to somewhat bear the hallucinations and keep some of her sanity intact by repeatedly convincing herself they weren't real, and that once she'd be out of here things would be okay. And this worked; until the day Lucy appeared. She hadn't seen Lucy in years, and it was the first time she'd felt relief in what felt like forever. And that relief was crushed as quickly as it came when she saw [basically gross horror shit happened to her body i'm too tired for details here you get the point].

It was at that point that the surrounding Nihilium fully seeped its way into her; she started frantically clawing at her eyes, trying to tear the image off her retina, but to no avail. Even completely blinded, she could still see all her loved ones.

She snapped. Broke into a frenzy, and eventually found her way out, causing significant damage to Avalia on the way. But she didn't care to destroy it. All she cared for now was finding Lucy. She'd never actually seen Lucy die. They were separated, and she never saw Lucy again. Initially, she'd assumed she'd been exiled with the other adults; after all, Lucy was older than Hakti. But now... She could feel her. Somewhere.

Her blindness had granted her a new sense, and she set out to find Lucy. Thanks to her skill with manipulating air, she could sense differences in currents, temperature and pressure to 'see' with her skin. She'd also rhythmically tap her sword against the ground in order to be able to detect more stationary objects through the compression waves generated by its sound.

And she's been wandering ever since. Trying to follow the extremely faint traces of her friend.  
And killing any who stand in her way.

### Circumstances
Hakti can be encountered from the start of the game, even though her difficulty is closer to lategame scaling. She can be found in Lumina, which is the home of the Amazons, Shamans, and the now eradicated Saeris Clan; and it is also the general location where Umbra first appears.

There are at least 3 other locations where she can spawn, with a 4th appearing after defeating Aosi.

Hakti has a number of behaviors with regards to what happened with Luscerna beforehand:

- If you've killed Luscerna before defeating Hakti, she will mistake you for Luscerna, and then brush you off as an impostor; thinking you're one of her delusions.
    - Defeating Hakti and attempting to spare her in this scenario leads to her committing suicide.
- If you've cleansed (the Mercy variant for beings afflicted with Nihilium) Luscerna before defeating Hakti, cleansing Hakti will become an option.
    - Hakti will mistake you for Luscerna, but say that something is off about you and she needs to 'fix what they did to you;' the fight will proceed as normal but her dialogue is altered signiicantly.
    - Cleansing Hakti leads to a small sidequest where you can learn about the Saeris Clan. She never mentions Luscerna by name, only calling her "Lucy." After realizing you're not Lucy, she will comment on how much your 'scent' resembles hers.
- If you haven't encountered Luscerna before defeating Hakti, sparing her will make her ask you to leave her alone.
    - Hakti will from now on only be findable in the location where you defeated her.
    - Trying to talk to her will make her tell you to leave.
        - Repeatedly talking to her will make her blow you away with a gust of wind and temporarily block off the entrance.
    - Defeating any Vortex that isn't Luscerna and returning to Hakti leads to you finding her dead.
        - The same happens when killing Luscerna after sparing Hakti.
    - Cleansing Luscerna in this state allows you to return to Hakti and cleanse her as well.
