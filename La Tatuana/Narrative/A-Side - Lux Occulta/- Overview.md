
<center><h1>La Tatuana</h1><h4>Lux Occulta</h4></center>

---
**Lux Occulta** is the first half of the La Tatuana story. It follows **Alma Santos** as she tries to establish herself as a force of nature that goes after criminals, and **Havushe Neziri**, the genius Interpol detective who is enlisted to hunt after her.

Alma, having been traumatized at youth by watching her best and only friend **Mikayla Stratos** be murdered before her very eyes, is given a second chance when she meets a mysterious masked lady, who introduces herself as **Akira**.

Akira teaches Alma that underneath the physics of the world lies an intricate system that can be controlled through consciousness. Through this, Alma develops several abilities, one of which she uses to very convincingly fake her death.

Alma has two primary goals.

First, she wants revenge for her best friend. Initially, she just wants to take out the two guys responsible, but after a very bloody search for the culprits, she finds out that they are already dead. In the midst of her frustration, she is offered a job to work as an assassin for an Italian crime family, **Petrucci**. 

Second, she wants to find her mother Ixchel, who is an extremely low profile criminal who leads the **Yaxche Syndicate**; headquartered in *Totonicapán, Guatemala*. Alma knows nothing about her mother; only her name. She has a single picture of her mother when she was young, and is the spitting image of her. 

Havushe wants to catch Alma and is afforded a level of resources no Interpol agent prior to her has ever been granted. She vastly outclasses Alma on an intellectual level, even quickly operating under the assumption that the culprit must possess extraordinary technology or supernatural abilities from the get-go. Havushe's strategy primarily revolves around testing and understanding the capabilities of the culprit before truly moving towards an arrest, because she cannot reason about that which she does not understand.

When bodies start turning up all over the globe with a suspiciously lacking amount of evidence, this catches the attention of not only the news, but also of Interpol.

Colloquially dubbed the "Cold Case Killer," reporters speak of cases where dozens upon dozens of victims are being found, and the only connecting MO is that there is no evidence towards their murder whatsoever, such that their cases are bound to go cold.

This new serial killer's file gets dropped onto the desk of Interpol detective Havushe Neziri, a genius who's renowned within her field for being able to crack the most impenetrable cases. Havushe initially refuses, on the premise that there is no such thing as a murder without evidence; either forensics is botching the job, or they're dealing with ghosts, neither of which are part of her job description.

However, she is goaded into taking the case after all by her colleague, who simply admires her solving the most complicated cases.

This serial killer eventually starts leaving calling cards containing an image of half an angel face with a demon's horn, and the other half being a skull with a halo. Underneath the skull reads the text "La Tatuana" in cursive, as if to taunt their investigators.

"La Tatuana" refers to an old Guatemalan folk tale, of a woman who got incarcerated on grounds of being a witch, and escaped prison by drawing a boat on the wall and sailing away on it.

To Havushe, this confirms a few things;

- At least a few of these murders were done by the same person
- This person either is female, or identifies with a female character
- They are taunting their investigators, showing a level of ego
- They are trying to create the impression that they are above the law
- They are trying to intimidate by expressing they may have supernatural capabilities

She also noted that all the victims had a dark history of their own, and this indicated that the killer may be a sort of vigilante, enacting their own idea of justice where they believed the justice system had failed.

A cat and mouse game ensues, cut similarly to Pulp Fiction; scenes alternate between past, present and future, with no indication of which is which. Some scenes depict Havushe piecing together evidence, and are immediately followed by tween Alma sitting on the couch watching MTV. These scenes are deliberately meant to obfuscate who La Tatuana actually is.

The title she assumes carries a Hispanic connotation, however, her mother, Ixchel Mendoza, is the spitting image of her, and we see images of Ixchel during the scenes where Alma is young, introducing a red herring where La Tatuana may be Ixchel.

The reality is, of course, that La Tatuana is Alma Santos; an unassuming foreign girl from a Venezuelan father (Humberto Santos) and Guatemalan mother (Ixchel Mendoza) living in the United Kingdom.

Ixchel's side of the family is from a background of drug dealing and gang violence, and when Alma's grandfather was murdered, she and Humberto decided to ship Alma off to live in Europe. They wished for her to have a better life than what she would've experienced in Venezuela.

Ixchel stayed behind because she is too high profile a target. Her daughter was still a secret from enemies of her gang (the Yaxche Syndicate), but it was only a matter of time until they found out. By having Humberto act out cheating on her, and faking a breakup, they then took him off of the spotlight, and he escaped (with her funding) to the United Kingdom.

However, once there, Ixchel could not keep sending him money, as that might compromise the entire plan. She used some trusted contacts to arrange him a job and a place to live in a relatively well-off neighborhood, but he was on his own from there.

Alma had a hard time adapting to her new environment. Her school was full of posh, wealthy children, and she was a foreigner, whose dad could only barely get by to afford the house that was above their stature.

Being rowdy and tough-spirited, she would often get into fights with the other students, which would ultimately mean trouble for _her_, because the kids would only stand up for each other, making her look like the perpetrator.

This led to an incident where she eventually had to change schools, for breaking the nose of one of the boys, after he had attempted to steal one of her gemstones, which was her favourite hobby.

In the new school, she faced much of the same issues as before, but tried to control herself a little so her dad wouldn't get in any further trouble, since she respected him. It was in this school where she also finally met someone in a similar situation as her; Mikaela Stratos, a red-headed Greek immigrant, whose parents also could barely afford to get by, and used most of their money to send their only daughter to the best school they can.

Alma and Mikaela quickly became best friends; they'd often go to Mikaela's house, and watch old Noire films on their ancient black and white TV.

Mikaela never got birthday presents, though her parents always made an exorbitant meal for her. Alma wished to change that, so she begged her dad to buy a present for Mikaela's upcoming eleventh birthday. Her dad was barely scraping by as it is, so he told her she had to work for it if she wanted to earn money, and she did.

Extra chores around the house, washing dad's car, taking care of the lawn. Anything she could do for money, she did, and she worked quite hard for a whole month, making enough to buy Mikaela a pendant of her favourite gemstone; Tanzanite.

The reality was that her father had taken out a loan from a local loanshark, to avoid getting in hot water with the banks. He was truly already in debt when she had asked, but he wished to help her so desperately, he took out a little extra.

But as the months passed, his job was giving him less and less hours, and he wasn't able to pay it back.

It eventually culminated in Alma getting abducted whilst playing outside; with Mikaela. Because Mikaela was with her, she got taken as well, so as to avoid leaving behind any witnesses.

The kidnappers argued among each other over whether they should've taken the other girl, since this job was only meant to scare her father, not cause a panic in the neighborhood. After their arguing intensified, one of the killers eventually grabbed Mikaela's head, and put a bullet through it unceremoniously, saying "Problem solved." and walking away.

This all happened right in front of Alma, who eventually was rescued when her father had put together the money. Mikaela's parents had also come, but unfortunately, all they could do was collect their daughters' corpse.

This experienced shattered Alma. Mikaela's parents, who were devastated, did not blame Alma or even her father for the ordeal. They gave her Mikaela's pendant, to have something to remember her by. Alma has held onto this pendant the whole time.

She fell into a deep depression around age 12. Her father's extreme guilt made him unwilling to force her to go to school or really do much of anything, so he enabled her to become a shut-in in her room, doing nothing but scrolling social media, watching series and movies, and playing videogames.

Eventually, he convinced her to go into therapy, but this didn't help much. Alma's PTSD developed into her experiencing severe psychoses, having visual and auditory hallucinations, and regular, extreme panic attacks. She never recovered, and only seemed to get worse as the years went by.

This was until she had acquired a new hallucination. A mysterious, masked woman in foreign robes, that she'd sometimes see around the premises. She didn't think much of it at first; after all, hallucinations were common, and she would get new ones every so often, that tended to linger.

But hallucinations don't usually ring the doorbell when she's home alone. They usually relate to things she's seen before, but this was different.

This masked lady said she had seen Alma's pain, and said she could perhaps offer a way to ameliorate it. Alma was naturally skeptical, and so the lady took her directly to her own Ripple; a dying land known as Eiare.

Alma was positively convinced she was going insane, so she brought back an object from Eiare, and showed it to her father, who asked her what it was. This confirmed to her that this was no hallucination.

Akira is ultimately the one who offered to teach Alma about Qualia, and help her develop the abilities she might need to get the only thing she believed could still her troubled heart; revenge.

Over several years, Alma trained with Akira, and learned about Qualia, Injectors, and how to engage in combat using Akira's "Squall" fighting style.

When she felt ready, she was finally left to her own devices, and started enacting her plan.

First, she utilised Vespera's Shadow Double to fake her own death. She figured it would be easier on her father if he believed she had killed herself, than that if she had become a murderer. She then set out on her path to find the people who had killed Mikaela, so she could kill them herself.

Her initial few days were rough. She had nowhere to go, and so she ended up spending a few nights in the hideout Mikaela's killers kept her in, because she couldn't think of a better place to stay. During that time, some teenagers ended up visiting on a dare, and she utilised her Injectors for the first time on another human; to scare them shitless. She really makes sure to make them feel like they're going to die there before letting them go, to ensure they never come back to this place again.

This is what originated Alma's idea to present herself as a horror monster who haunts her victims.

Her lack of money was a problem, and so she resolved to steal from criminals. She knew the shady areas around, and would track down people she'd identify as criminals (drug dealers, robbers, and so on), and haphazardly do a "haunting" on them; taking their wallets in the process.

It was through these shady areas that she managed to find information about a guy people called [Nasty William](William%20Redpath.md). He's one of the local brokers who has eyes and ears all over the city. She went to visit him, and he readily divulged to her that [Ross](Ross%20Petrucci.md) of the [Petrucci Family](Petrucci%20Family.md) is in town, and that they were the ones the killers worked for.

Alma goes to the location indicated by William, but only finds three of Ross' goons there. Upon being questioned, they gave her a hard time, and the situation escalated; this is where Alma committed her first murders. Fortunately, she found some information on one of their corpses that led her to where the actual Ross could be found; and ended up having to kill a dozen more before finally cornering him at a hangar.

After a spectacular sequence where Alma tears through another two dozen henchmen, she is finally face to face with Ross himself, who now has nowhere to go. Ross ends up divulging to her that the two guys who killed her friend have themselves been killed. They had botched the job, and made the Petruccis look real bad. Like they just killed kids over nothing. So the boss had them offed soon after.

In response, Alma shot Ross in the face. She then emptied the entire magazine into his lifeless corpse in frustration. Everything she did up to this point was for nothing.

She sat with her feelings for a while, in the bloodbath she'd created.

That is when she heard a noise, and noticed someone was approaching. But by this point, depression had set back into Alma. She'd faked her death. She'd given up everything, only to find out the guys had been dead long ago. Now she'd murdered at least 40 people. All for nothing. She was ready to accept what was about to happen.

Instead, only a single man appeared, who appraised the scene with bemused awe. This man calmly addressed Alma, asking her if she was the one who caused this scene. She affirmed simply. After a moment of silence, she asked if he's here to kill her.

He said that, judging by the state of this scene, it's more likely that she would kill him. He's surprised he's still standing. His callous demeanour confused her. He seemed to be completely at peace with the prospect that he could die at a moment's notice. She remained silent.

He then sat down in front of her, lit a cigarette, and calmly asked her what the fuck had happened here.

Having nothing to lose, Alma told him the entire story. Mikayla's death, her own depression, Akira's appearance, her learning to use Qualia, everything. The man listened to her entire story, lighting cigarettes back to back as she spoke.

When she finished, he simply asked her "What now?"

She said she didn't know.

That is when he offered her to work for him. Introducing himself as [Antonio Petrucci](Antonio%20Petrucci.md), 
he explained that she had killed one of his cousins and his goons. Whilst the family is not going to be happy about this, he believes he can put in a good word for her, and have her work as one of their assassins. In fact, he believes her to be the perfect assassin.

In return, she can take out scum just like the two that killed Mikayla, and she will live an otherwise comfortable life and be paid well.

After some deliberation, Alma agreed, and her partnership with the Petruccis began.

It was through this partnership that Alma developed two assassination styles, which she dubbed "**The Red Herring**" and "**The Ghost**."

**The Red Herring** involved her setting up a kill to look like it was done by someone else. This allowed the Petruccis to stir the pot in one place as a distraction from something else, or to shine a negative light on their enemies.

**The Ghost** is the sort of perfect kill that La Tatuana became famous for. The only evidence is the lack of evidence. This is particularly potent in the criminal world, because they will clean up the crime scenes of their own people, as they don't want police attention in any form whatsoever.

Alma's clean killings didn't start surfacing until after she'd parted ways with the Petruccis.

Initially, her disagreements with how they treated her and desire for independence led to her setting up a service where one could "book a murder," but unlike regular assassination services, this would be free. The cost, instead, is that the target being evaluated is of interest to the assassin.

Alma still wished to rid the world of scum, yet now the only scum she was killing were enemies of the Petruccis. She wanted bigger fish, from a bigger pond. 

Once the Petruccis discovered this service, they started doubting Alma's loyalty. Alma had come out of the shadows, and her murders were now appearing on the news. This was bad for business.

Furthermore, the Petruccis were quite concerned about Alma. Sure, she co-operated with them now, but her reputation had grown, and it had become quite evident that, were she to turn on them, they couldn't actually do anything to stop her.

This delicate balance was disturbed by Tony Petrucci himself, who was catching a lot of the flak for her misbehaviour. He suggested she part ways with the family, as he had grown quite fond of her. After all, he was the one she'd most spoken to within the family.

Alma agreed, and went her own way, considering Tony to be an ally despite his crooked background. She'd since come to understand that the criminal world wasn't simply full of bad guys, and that bad guys themselves had different categories of bad. After all, what was she but a mass murderer?

With the wealth and resources she'd amassed from the Petruccis, she now began building her own empire; starting with [The Directory](Numbering.md), a list of people, objects, creatures, and organizations of note that she has gathered information on.
