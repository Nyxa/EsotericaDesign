So for context; this is an ability of the protagonist of my second story, La Tatuana, which is pretty tied to the first story, Halithell

Basically, the game I'm working on is part of a larger narrative I hope to have the fortune to be able to complete in my lifetime, where Halithell is kind of like the starting point, La Tatuana is adjacent and happening simultaneously, Axiom Empress is a sort of prequel, and then finally there's Halithell: Altire where everything comes together and resolves in a big climactic war

This is important because while the protagonist is from La Tatuana, this particular ability only gets used once, in Halithell: Altire, as the conclusion to her story

The second piece of context is how abilities work. Normally Injectors are abilities you develop and have a good amount of control over what they will eventually become, limited only by your natural aptitudes and tendencies. However, Alma is Attuned to Ethereon, which works differently. Since that is the Qualia of Concepts, it's virtually impossible to actually control its outcome. 

Instead of a typical Injector, Ethereon users produce a "Projection" which is like a piece of themselves turned into an ability. That Projection usually takes the form of some kind of familiar, either a creature or person or even an object, and it can be "activated" to become the actual ability it is.

Essentially, while most Injectors have very hard rules about precisely which laws of physics they can break, Ethereon works more like a soft magic system, where you can make your abilities more powerful by imposing more severe restrictions, and can bend more or less any physical lawsto enact the ability, relative to your aptitude with it. They also have "passive" abilities in their manifest form, which allows you to get some benefit without needing to fulfill activation conditions. 

All projections have a name, as well as a name for their activation, which is also something unique to Ethereon. 

Alma has exactly three Projections:
- Hand of Antithesis
- Hand of Reprisal
- Hand of Serenity

##### Hand of Antithesis
This Projection has two doll-sized manifestations: a grumpy Goth angel girl named **Aurora**, and a bubbly pastel demon named **Vespera**. 

Despite there being two of them, they still share a single activation condition, and are a single Projection, even though they act independently from one another. They speak and move freely. 

They each have their own passive effects. 

Aurora can transform into information tools, such as a gun sight that lets her see through walls, or a radar which shows the names of those around her. 

Vespera can transform into stealth and deception tools, such as a beacon which lets her port between shaded areas, or a complete physical body double. 

Their activation is called **Lacryma Dea**.

It requires her to be split into two halves of roughly equal measure. These halves will then rejoin, and she will take up her new form. 

In this state, Alma gains extreme mental clarity, and Vespera and Aurora become two long blades. 

Aurora a blade of pure light, which may create sources of light upon striking the air. Vespera becomes a mere hilt, which grows into a blade  after being dipped into shadows. The shadow blade lasts for 3 seconds, and will cause physical damage by striking at the shadows of other objects. 

The shadow blade can additionally absorb any amount of impact when blocking. These impacts are cumulatively returned on the next strike of the light blade. 

Lastly, she gains a small dagger made of pure ice. Inserting this into anything will freeze and then shatter that object into precisely 337 pieces. 

##### Hand of Reprisal
A "fetish, " which is an Injector that's imbued onto a physical object. In this case, it's the tanzanite pendant that belonged to her friend, Mikayla, whom she witnessed the murder of when she was only 10, which essentially kickstarts her narrative. Her murder happened on the 3rd of March in 2007, which is relevant for later. 

Its manifest form is a small doll that resembles Mikayla, causing Alma to permanently keep the doll's face hidden, usually with some kind of bag.

The doll is named **Vindicta**, and does not ever move or speak. Its only passive ability is that it can provide the activation conditions in its pendant form. 

Its activation conditions require Alma to squeeze the pendant, which causes the pendant to start oozing blood. This blood will then arrange itself into a poem onto whatever surface it spilled on. This poem is a riddle, the solution of which leads to a list of arbitrary instructions. These may include jumping on the Eiffel tower, saving a puppy, stealing a loaf of bread, killing 100 people, spinning in a wheat field in Egypt, etc. 

Completing the entire list of instructions makes Alma eligible to activate Hand of Reprisal. 

Its activation is called **Angelus Mortis**. 

Activating it completely changes her appearance, and enhances all her other abilities. She instantly regenerates completely, Hand of Antithesis becomes a set of powerful weapons, she gains extreme physical speed, and access to a sniper rifle made of ice, containing a single bullet, which will ignore any matter except that of her "Target," and has a range of 33 kilometers. Using the rifle immediately ends the ability; otherwise, it lasts for precisely 7 minutes. 

##### Hand of Serenity
A creepy blue girl with her mouth sewn shut into a permanent wide smile, and her lower half (including her belly) being a medical cart on wheels. She does move and appears to have thoughts, but she does not speak. Her cart may contain useful medical supplies such as bandages and ethanol, at times. 

Her name is **Caerulea**. 

Her passive ability is that Alma will always regenerate all damage, but at a normal rate. She can lose her arm, and it will grow back over 5 to 10 years. She does not reduce any pain felt from injury. 

As long as a single atom of Alma remains, she can reform completely, given enough time. 

Her activation requires Alma to have at most a tenth of her blood left, have died, and then drink at least a drop of blood of someone else. 

Her activation is called **Cruora**. 

This ability transforms her into something monstrous, with blood oozing from every orifice, and her mouth gaining an appendage with which she can drain targets of blood. During this, she has extreme physical speed and strength, no mental acuity whatsoever, and will have no memories of the event. 

Drinking blood from external sources restores her wounds, and replenishes her own blood. The form lasts until she completely bleeds out, and dies.
