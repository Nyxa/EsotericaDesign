Also called **Filthy William**, he is a broker who can connect big name criminals with each other, and ensure their business operates smoothly. He also engages in "clean-up services," for when business goes south.

His job requires him to have eyes and ears all over the region, making him fairly knowledgeable on all the dealings that happen there.

He is a middle-aged black man, well-put-together, who likes to wear a fancy suit, and walks with a cane for his atrophied right leg. He often wears sunglasses, even indoors, and speaks with a posh accent, and a deep, gravelly voice. He is very polite, charming, and generally compassionate. However, he is called Nasty William for a reason. His business goes smoothly because he likes to ensure no one gets on his bad side.
